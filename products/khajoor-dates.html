Aseel Khajoor Kya Hai?

Aseel Sindh ki sab se mashhoor khajoor ki qism hai — khaas tor par Khairpur ke khajoor ke baaghon ki. Darmiyane size ki, naram, gehre bhoore rang ki, aur mithas mein mutawazin. Pakistan mein jo khajur sab se zyada khai aur bheji jati hai, wo yehi hai. Ye fresh wali qism hai: saabit dates seedha 5kg box mein, sukha kar sakht aur cheeni mein lipet kar nahi.

Khairpur Se Shikarpur Tak

Khajoor Sindh mein kisi bhi achar se kahin purani cheez hai. Khairpur ke khajoor ke baagh usi ilaqay mein hain jahan Shikarpur hai, aur mausam mein ghar walay khajur packet ke bajaye bori ke hisaab se kharidte hain — iftar ke liye, mehmanon ke liye, aur shehron mein rishtedaron ko bhejne ke liye. 5kg box mein bechna yahan ka purana tareeqa hai: saabit, khuli aur taza.

Zaiqa Aur Khane Ka Tareeqa

Fresh aseel khajoor dry dates se naram hoti hai aur itni meethi nahi lagti ke zyada ho jaye. Mithas gol aur caramel jaisi hai, chilka aasani se dabta hai, aur gooda guthli tak naram rehta hai. Do teen khajoor ek glass doodh ke saath yahan ka aam tareeqa hai — sehri mein, iftar mein, ya subah chai ke saath. Badam ya akhrot bhar kar, milkshake mein blend kar ke, kheer aur halwe mein daal kar, ya cheeni ki jagah bhi istemal hoti hai.

Rakhne Ka Tareeqa

Box ko thandi, khushk jagah par band rakhein, dhoop se door. Garmi mein ya box khulne ke baad fridge mein rakhna behtar hai — isse dates naram rehti hain aur sukhti nahi. Hamesha saaf, khushk haath ya chamach istemal karein.

Kis Ke Liye Behtar Hai

Agar ghar mein khajoor regular chalti hai — khaas kar Ramzan mein — to 5kg box har hafte chhote packet kharidne se kaafi sasta parta hai. Dukanon, daftaron, aur gift box bhejne walon ke liye bhi munasib hai.
